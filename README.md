# **Customer Segmentation with Clustering**

<div align="justify">
  
## 📝 **Objectives**   
To apply statistical and machine learning (ML) methods to customer segmentation with clustering techniques. 
The project involves the following steps:

* exploration of the data set
* preprocessing the data and conducting feature engineering
* determination of the optimal number of clusters ($k$)
* application of ML models to reduce dimensions and segment customers.


## 💡 **Business Context**
> Understanding and serving customers are two of the retail industry's most important strategic marketing objectives.
> Knowing the customer allows businesses to be more customer-centric with improvements to metrics such as
> marketing efficiency, enhanced product development, increased customer satisfaction, better customer retention,
> price optimisation, and strategic resource allocation.

> Customer segmentation allows a business to group customers based on demographics (e.g. age, gender, education, occupation, marital status, family size),
> geographics (e.g. country, transportation, preferred language), psychographics (e.g. lifestyle, values, personality, attitudes),
> behaviour (e.g. purchase history, brand loyalty, response to marketing activities), technographic (e.g. device type, browser type, original source), and
> needs (e.g. product features, service needs, delivery method).

## 📊 **Data Pre-processing Approaches**
* Assessed and treated for missing values and duplicates
* Performed feature engineering to create 5 new features, based on the suggested marketing perspective 

## 🚀 **Methods Implemented**

* Customer segmentation was performed using unsupervised ML methods, including *k-means* and *hierarchical clustering*.
* Dimensionality reduction of the clustered dataset was performed using principal component analysis (PCA) and t-distributed stochastic 
neighbour embedding (t-SNE).

## 📌 **Detailed Analysis**

A report outlining the analytical insights derived, explaining the approach undertaken for customer segmentation
is provided.
